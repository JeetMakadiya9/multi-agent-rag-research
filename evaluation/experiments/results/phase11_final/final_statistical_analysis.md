# Statistical analysis

For paired binary correctness among 176 labeled claims with valid predictions from both B and C, exact two-sided McNemar uses B-only correct=1, C-only correct=1, 2 discordant pairs. p=1.0000; no statistical significance at alpha=.05. Small discordance limits precision; no ranking or superiority inference is made.

The results are a full DEV descriptive evaluation, not an independent test-set evaluation.
